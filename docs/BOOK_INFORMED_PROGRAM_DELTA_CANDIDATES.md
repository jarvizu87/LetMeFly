## Black Crown Revised v2.1 — approved Block-5 lateral-glute amendment

The earlier book review identified hip abduction as a distinct lateral-glute role that was not directly represented in Black Crown Block 5. The v2.0 source had deliberately decided against an additional loaded glute slot. The athlete subsequently made an explicit program-edit decision to improve that specialization architecture.

The resulting v2.1 change is deliberately **replacement-only**, not additive.

### Exact prescription change

Only **Weeks 25–29, Day 5** change:

- retain primary Chest-Supported Row 4 x 8–10;
- retain Lat Pulldown 2–3 x 8–10;
- replace the later secondary Chest-Supported Row 2 x 8–10 support slot with **Machine Hip Abduction 2 x 15–25 @ RPE 7–8**;
- rest **45–60 seconds**;
- equipment fallback order: **Seated Band Hip Abduction**, then **Mini-Band Lateral Walk**;
- no new session, no extra support slot, and no added total support-set count.

Week 30 remains the existing non-max check and is unchanged.

### Why this is now permitted

The external glute material did not override v2.0 on its own. The change became legitimate only after an intentional athlete/program-design decision created **Black Crown v2.1**. The old v2.0 source remains the immutable historical base; v2.1 is a governed amendment layered on top.
