# LetMeFly Color Harmonization Standard v1

## Purpose

Make the approved Home mockup the visual north star for the rest of LetMeFly without discarding the newer layouts or the purple Raizen/Fenrir energy language.

## Locked hierarchy

1. **Black / charcoal / steel** — primary surfaces, framing, cards, navigation, and data UI.
2. **Red** — primary brand/environment/action accent. Use for active navigation, primary actions, selected tabs, important labels, program/week selection, progress highlights, library selection, and the Home-style environmental glow.
3. **Purple** — secondary gamification/energy accent. Use for Raizen/Fenrir lightning, runes, fantasy energy, selected milestone flourish, or restrained identity detailing. Purple should not dominate every border, button, or active state.
4. **White / gray** — information hierarchy and legibility.
5. Existing exercise imagery remains grounded and instructional; the surrounding LetMeFly frame may use restrained red/black treatment, with purple only as a subtle secondary energy tint.

## Tab treatment

- **Home** — authoritative approved red/black mountain + wolf reference. Do not recolor it toward purple.
- **Train** — practical workout execution; keep existing exercise images readable. Red/black UI framing, restrained secondary purple only where identity/gamification calls for it.
- **Program** — red active/week/program selection and environmental glow; purple may remain in cinematic energy artwork.
- **Progress** — red analytics/selected-state language; purple limited to identity/world art accents.
- **Exercises** — red active filters and card focus; exact existing exercise images stay unmodified at their core.
- **Coach** — red actionable controls and context emphasis; purple remains in restrained Raizen/Fenrir identity art.
- **Profile** — red character-sheet chrome and Goal Tracker emphasis; Raizen/Fenrir art may keep purple lightning/runes.
- **More** — red menu/action emphasis on the black/steel base; purple only for special identity details.

## Safety and semantics

Red is also used for warning, pain/injury, destructive, and safety-critical semantics. Those states must remain clearly distinguishable through copy, iconography, contrast, and context rather than color alone.

## Non-negotiable behavior boundary

This is a presentation standard only. It must not alter Crownforge or Black Crown prescriptions, sets/reps/loading, progression rules, workout logging or recovery, private athlete data, Training Max ownership, Exercise Intelligence, Coach decision logic, or sync behavior.
