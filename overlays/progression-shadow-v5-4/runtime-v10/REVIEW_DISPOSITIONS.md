# Runtime v10 Review Disposition Contract

Finalized pilot evidence remains immutable.

A technically healthy finalized review may be permanently excluded from the visible-pilot sample only when the independent human review was entered incorrectly:

- `HUMAN_REVIEW_ERROR`
- `INVALID_RELOAD_CONFIRMATION`

The original receipt, machine evidence, human review, and finalized packet remain intact. The disqualified workout no longer counts toward the three-workout pilot minimum, so a replacement real workout is required.

A disposition is invalid and cannot hide the workout when any technical condition is unhealthy, including progression processing, mapping compatibility, replay idempotency, Main Quest completion, readiness resolution, Yellow decision coverage, original-hook receipt consistency, first-awarded XP consistency, or provenance.

Dispositions are add-once records in the private pilot journal.
